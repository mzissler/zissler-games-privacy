Axolotl Math Battle – Support

Need help with Axolotl Math Battle or want to report a problem?

Open a request at https://github.com/mzissler/zissler-games-privacy/issues and the developer will respond.

FAQ

How do I play? Pick LEVEL 1, LEVEL 2 or LEVEL 3, then ADDING, SUBTRACTING or MIXED, and tap START BATTLE! When a monster gets ready to attack, tap the answer on the number pad and press OK. Each right answer lets Bubbles the axolotl hit back. You have 5 seconds for each problem.

How do I win a battle? Get 10 right answers to beat the monster. A wrong answer or running out of time costs one of your 3 hearts. Your hearts fill back up before every new battle, and each new monster is a little tougher.

What is the Bubble Blast? Answer 3 problems quickly (in 3 seconds or less) to charge the Bubble Blast. Your next right answer does double damage.

Does Bubbles change? Yes! Every 5 battles won, Bubbles evolves into a new form, up to a third, dragon-like form.

What kind of problems are there? You choose adding only, subtracting only or a mix of both, with whole numbers, and every number and answer is 20 or less (never negative). Level 1 uses single digits only, Level 2 mixes in some teen numbers, and Level 3 uses any numbers up to 20. Each level and operation has its own top-10 high-score table, ranked by battles won, then right answers.

How do I turn the sound off? Tap the speaker button. The game remembers your choice.

How do I clear the high scores? Delete and reinstall the app.

Does the game need the internet? No, it plays fully offline.

Does the game collect data? No. See the privacy policy.
