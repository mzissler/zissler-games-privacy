Grappler – Support

Need help with Grappler or want to report a problem?

Open a request at https://github.com/mzissler/zissler-games-privacy/issues (a parent or guardian, please) and the developer will respond.

FAQ

How do I reset progress? Delete and reinstall the app.

How do I win a submission? Armbar: tap when the needle is in the zone. Rear Naked Choke: tap fast. Kneebar: hold the button and let go in the zone. Every boss has a weak spot shown on its intro card.

Does the game need the internet? No, it plays fully offline.

Does the game collect data? No. See the privacy policy.
