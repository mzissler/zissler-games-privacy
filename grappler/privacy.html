Grappler – Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Grappler is a game for kids. We designed it to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Grappler does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases, no chat and no links out of the app.

Data saved on your device

The game remembers which bosses you have beaten, the belts you earned and how many stars you got. That progress is saved only on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is removed when you delete the app.

Children

The app is made for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): because no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics services.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? A parent or guardian can reach the developer through the support page.
