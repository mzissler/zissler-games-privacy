Mog Daddy: Hair Check – Support

Need help with Mog Daddy: Hair Check or want to report a problem?

Open a request at https://github.com/mzissler/zissler-games-privacy/issues and the developer will respond.

FAQ

How do I reset progress? Delete and reinstall the app.

How do I get MOG DADDY? Comb every lock along the dotted ghost lines until they all turn green, tap away every stray hair, match the volume shown on the hair goal card (use HEAD FLIP), then hit LOCK IN early for the speed bonus.

Something keeps messing up my hair! Tap the dog, your little brother or the seagull to shoo them before they reach you. After a gust of wind, a vent or a fan, comb the hair back into place.

Does the game need the internet? No, it plays fully offline.

Does the game collect data? No. See the privacy policy.
