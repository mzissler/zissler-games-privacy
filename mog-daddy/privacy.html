Mog Daddy: Hair Check – Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Mog Daddy: Hair Check is a casual hair-styling game. We designed it to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Mog Daddy: Hair Check does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases, no chat and no links out of the app.

Data saved on your device

The game remembers which spots you have unlocked, your stars and your best aura scores. That progress is saved only on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics services.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Reach the developer through the support page.
