Lost Halls: Ghost Pirate Maze – Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Lost Halls: Ghost Pirate Maze is a spooky maze game. We designed it to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Lost Halls does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases, no chat, no network access and no links out of the app.

Data saved on your device

The game keeps best-times tables (the 3-letter initials a player types and their escape times) and the sound on/off setting. These are saved only on your device, are never sent anywhere, and are removed when you delete the app. The initials are not linked to any name, account or device identifier.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics services.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Reach the developer through the support page.
