Lost Halls: Ghost Pirate Maze – Support

Need help with Lost Halls: Ghost Pirate Maze or want to report a problem?

Open a request at https://github.com/mzissler/zissler-games-privacy/issues and the developer will respond.

FAQ

How do I play? Drag on the left side of the screen to walk and on the right side to look around. Hold SPRINT to run, but watch your stamina.

How do I escape a level? Find every key on the level, then go to the exit door.

How do I get away from the ghost pirate? Captain Gloombeard wanders the halls with his lantern. Hide in a locker and stay still, or throw a bottle to make him go check the noise. Running is loud, and on later levels he can hear you walking too.

What happens if he catches me? You lose one of your 3 lives and start the level again from its entrance. Lose all 3 and it's game over.

How many levels are there? Three: The Yellow Rooms, The Flooded Halls and The Captain's Deep. Every maze is new each time you play.

How do the best times work? Each level, plus a full escape of all 3 levels from the start, has its own top-10 list with 3-letter initials. To clear them, delete and reinstall the app.

How do I turn the sound off? Tap the speaker button.

Does the game need the internet? No, it plays fully offline.

Does the game collect data? No. See the privacy policy.
