Army Guys: Math Mission – Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Army Guys: Math Mission is a first-grade math game made for kids ages 6–8. We designed it to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Army Guys: Math Mission does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases, no chat, no network access and no links out of the app.

Data saved on your device

The game keeps a high-score table (the 3-letter initials a player types, the number of problems solved, the area reached and accuracy) and a couple of settings, such as the last math mode chosen. These are saved only on your device, are never sent anywhere, and are removed when you delete the app. The initials are not linked to any name, account or device identifier.

Children

The app is made for children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's Kids category rules.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics services.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
