Army Guys: Math Mission – Support

Need help with Army Guys: Math Mission or want to report a problem?

Open a request at https://github.com/mzissler/zissler-games-privacy/issues and the developer will respond.

FAQ

How do I play? Pick LEVEL 1, LEVEL 2 or LEVEL 3, tap START MISSION, then READY! Tap the answer on the number pad and press OK. Every right answer marches your squad forward. You have 2 minutes.

What kind of problems are there? Every level mixes adding and subtracting with whole numbers, and every number and answer is 20 or less (never negative). Level 1 uses single digits only, Level 2 mixes in some teen numbers, and Level 3 uses any numbers up to 20. Each level has its own top-10 high-score table.

How do I move to the next area? Get 5 right answers in each area: Boot Camp, Jungle, River Crossing and The Bridge, then 7 at the Enemy Base. Capture the enemy flag with 27 right answers, then keep going for bonus points.

What are the counting dots? Little dots (and tens bars for teen numbers) under each problem that help with counting. Turn them on or off on the title screen.

How do I clear the high scores? Delete and reinstall the app.

Does the game need the internet? No, it plays fully offline and has no sound.

Does the game collect data? No. See the privacy policy.
