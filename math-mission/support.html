Army Guys: Math Mission – Support

Need help with Army Guys: Math Mission or want to report a problem?

Open a request at https://github.com/mzissler/zissler-games-privacy/issues and the developer will respond.

FAQ

How do I play? Pick ADDING, SUBTRACTING or MIXED, tap START MISSION, then READY! Tap the answer on the number pad and press OK. Every right answer marches your squad forward. You have 2 minutes.

What kind of problems are there? Adding and subtracting with whole numbers within 20. The answers are never negative.

How do I move to the next area? Get enough right answers in each area: Boot Camp, Jungle, River Crossing, The Bridge and Enemy Base. Capture the enemy flag with 19 right answers, then keep going for bonus points.

What are the counting dots? Little dots under each problem that help with counting. Turn them on or off on the title screen.

How do I clear the high scores? Delete and reinstall the app.

Does the game need the internet? No, it plays fully offline and has no sound.

Does the game collect data? No. See the privacy policy.
