Angry Elf: Christmas Chaos – Support

Need help with Angry Elf: Christmas Chaos or want to report a problem?

Open a request at https://github.com/mzissler/zissler-games-privacy/issues (a parent or guardian, please) and the developer will respond.

FAQ

How do I reset progress? Delete and reinstall the app.

How do I get away from Mom and Dad? Cause mischief (open presents, eat cookies, smash ornaments) to fill the FIT meter, then tap FIT! when a grown-up flashes "!" and reaches for you. You can also SLIDE next to a curtain, the tree, the pantry or the blanket fort to hide.

Christmas Eve is hard! Mom AND Dad are on bedtime duty, so you need two fits. Save up mischief before you reach Santa's cookies.

Does the game need the internet? No, it plays fully offline.

Does the game collect data? No. See the privacy policy.
